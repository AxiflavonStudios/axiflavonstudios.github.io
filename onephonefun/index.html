The games

Guess the Drawing

One player draws a secret word. The next watches it replayed and picks the answer.

Charades

Act it out without a sound while one player guesses and a judge keeps it honest.

Pocket Fart

Phone in your pocket, walk to the goal – move too much and it farts.

Loony Lines

Finish the same sentence from random word cards. The judge ranks the funniest.

Murderer

Some players are secretly Murderers. Find them and vote them out before it is too late.

Dilemma

Pick one of two options in secret. How well do your friends know you?

Illusionist

One player is secretly the Illusionist and must bluff their way through.

No Reaction

Read a joke to a friend, who must listen and keep a straight face.

List

Rank two or three items in secret while another player guesses your order.

Quiz

A host reads the questions, contestants climb the ladder, with lifelines to help.
