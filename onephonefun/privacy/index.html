Privacy Policy

One Phone Fun · Effective 7 November 2026

In short: One Phone Fun does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

One Phone Fun is made by Axiflavon Studios, run by Johan Inganni as a sole trader in Sweden. If you have any question about this policy, email axiflavonstudios@gmail.com.

What the app collects

Nothing. Specifically:

  • No accounts. You never sign up or log in.
  • No analytics or tracking. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not follow you across other apps or websites.
  • No ads.
  • Player names you type in to play stay in the phone's memory during the game only. They are never saved or sent anywhere, and they are gone when the app is closed.
  • Motion sensor. The game Pocket Fart reads the phone's motion sensor while it is being played, to notice movement. The readings are used on the phone in that moment only and are never recorded or sent anywhere.
  • No camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos. The app does not use any of them.

Purchases

The free trial and the full unlock are handled entirely by the App Store. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only asks the App Store, on your phone, whether the trial or the full version is active. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Network connections

The app itself connects to nothing on the internet. The only connection is Apple's own purchase system, which the App Store uses to confirm purchases.

Children

One Phone Fun is a party game for groups of friends and families. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data about you. Since One Phone Fun holds no personal data, there is nothing to access or delete, but you are always welcome to contact us. You also have the right to complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
