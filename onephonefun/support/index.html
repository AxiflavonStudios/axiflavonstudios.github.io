Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?

You can play every game free for one day, counted from the moment you start the trial. When the day is over the games are locked until you unlock the full version. Nothing is charged automatically, and there is no subscription.

I bought the full version. How do I get it on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Open the app and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose One Phone Fun.

How do I change the language?

The app starts in your phone's language. You can switch to any of the 20 languages from the language button on the start screen.

How many players do I need?

Each game shows how many players it supports when you set it up. Many games can be played by just two, and Murderer is made for bigger groups.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All games work offline. A connection is only needed to start the trial, buy the full version or restore a purchase.

Does the app collect my data?

No. See our Privacy Policy.
